Animal Communication Reading Set

Bees: Bees use a waggle dance to show food locations. If a bee finds rich flowers, it signals others immediately. This method is more effective than random searching.

Elephants: Elephants communicate using low-frequency sounds that can travel long distances. Scientists have studied these calls for many years.

Wolves: Wolves use howls and body language to organize pack activities and respond to danger.

Birds: Birds communicate with songs and warning calls. Young birds often learn songs from older members of the group.
